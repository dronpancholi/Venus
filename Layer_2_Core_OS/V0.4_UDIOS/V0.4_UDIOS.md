**Version**: 0.4

## 2. Modular Engine Index

UDIOS is composed of 28 core decision modules and 15 institutional output templates. Use the directory links below to navigate the complete operating system:

### 2.1 The 28 Decision Modules
1.  **[Module 01: Decision Intake Engine](./udios_modules/MODULE_01_DECISION_INTAKE.md)**  
    *Decision normalization protocols, parameter mappings, and schema specifications.*
2.  **[Module 02: Decision Classification Engine](./udios_modules/MODULE_02_DECISION_CLASSIFICATION.md)**  
    *Multi-vector routing (Strategic, Architectural, Infrastructure, AI, Security, Financial, Legal).*
3.  **[Module 03: Decision Importance Scoring](./udios_modules/MODULE_03_DECISION_IMPORTANCE_SCORING.md)**  
    *Blast radius formulas, reversibility classification, cost indices, and urgency calculations.*
4.  **[Module 04: Evidence Collection](./udios_modules/MODULE_04_EVIDENCE_COLLECTION.md)**  
    *Auto-retrieval strategies across academic papers, benchmark data, Git issues, and vendor specs.*
5.  **[Module 05: Evidence Quality Scoring](./udios_modules/MODULE_05_EVIDENCE_QUALITY_SCORING.md)**  
    *Mathematical weighting index (0-100) applied to gathered evidence to discount bias and marketing noise.*
6.  **[Module 06: Assumption Validation](./udios_modules/MODULE_06_ASSUMPTION_VALIDATION.md)**  
    *Mapping decision assumptions to empirical validation loops, risk metrics, and fallback policies.*
7.  **[Module 07: Alternative Generation](./udios_modules/MODULE_07_ALTERNATIVE_GENERATION.md)**  
    *Expanding standard options into comprehensive alternative vectors, explaining historical existence.*
8.  **[Module 08: Trade-off Analysis](./udios_modules/MODULE_08_TRADE_OFF_ANALYSIS.md)**  
    *Multi-criteria matrices balancing Latency, Cost, Maintainability, Security, Complexity, and Lock-in.*
9.  **[Module 09: Build vs. Buy](./udios_modules/MODULE_09_BUILD_VS_BUY.md)**  
    *Core mathematical framework evaluating custom engineering vs. commercial vendor software licensing.*
10. **[Module 10: Open Source Evaluation](./udios_modules/MODULE_10_OPEN_SOURCE_EVALUATION.md)**  
    *Vetting open-source codebases based on license types, CVE histories, maintenance rate, and API drift.*
11. **[Module 11: Vendor Evaluation](./udios_modules/MODULE_11_VENDOR_EVALUATION.md)**  
    *Evaluating third-party providers (Cloud, Payments, Identity, Storage) on compliance and SLA terms.*
12. **[Module 12: ADR Engine](./udios_modules/MODULE_12_ADR_ENGINE.md)**  
    *Automatic generation of Architecture Decision Records (ADRs) tied back to validation parameters.*
13. **[Module 13: Decision Debate](./udios_modules/MODULE_13_DECISION_DEBATE.md)**  
    *Structured consensus system between 10 specialized virtual persona roles.*
14. **[Module 14: AI Decision Board](./udios_modules/MODULE_14_AI_DECISION_BOARD.md)**  
    *Evaluating AI vs. traditional logic; selecting agents, fine-tuning, RAG, or local vs. cloud LLMs.*
15. **[Module 15: Economic Decision Engine](./udios_modules/MODULE_15_ECONOMIC_DECISION_ENGINE.md)**  
    *Unit economics, developer time allocations, gross margin thresholds, and compute cost models.*
16. **[Module 16: Scalability Decision Engine](./udios_modules/MODULE_16_SCALABILITY_DECISION_ENGINE.md)**  
    *Simulating performance thresholds from initial MVP (100 users) up to hyper-scale (100M+ users).*
17. **[Module 17: Risk Decision Engine](./udios_modules/MODULE_17_RISK_DECISION_ENGINE.md)**  
    *Probability-impact-recoverability matrix mapping risks to rollback budgets.*
18. **[Module 18: Complexity Budget](./udios_modules/MODULE_18_COMPLEXITY_BUDGET.md)**  
    *Limiting over-engineering by enforcing a standard logical complexity budget limit.*
19. **[Module 19: Opportunity Cost](./udios_modules/MODULE_19_OPPORTUNITY_COST.md)**  
    *Quantifying the economic and technical yield of rejected pathways and deferred roadmap features.*
20. **[Module 20: Future Evolution](./udios_modules/MODULE_20_FUTURE_EVOLUTION.md)**  
    *Predicting decision survival rates over 5-year and 10-year engineering lifecycles.*
21. **[Module 21: Institutional Memory](./udios_modules/MODULE_21_INSTITUTIONAL_MEMORY.md)**  
    *Logging post-decision incidents, regrets, and successes into active intake filtering parameters.*
22. **[Module 22: Decision Confidence](./udios_modules/MODULE_22_DECISION_CONFIDENCE.md)**  
    *Mathematical synthesis producing a final confidence percentage score (0-100%).*
23. **[Module 23: Decision Explainability](./udios_modules/MODULE_23_DECISION_EXPLAINABILITY.md)**  
    *Generating human-readable and agent-interpretable semantic explainability rationales.*
24. **[Module 24: Decision Approval Workflow](./udios_modules/MODULE_24_DECISION_APPROVAL_WORKFLOW.md)**  
    *Human-in-the-loop and multi-agent approval routing pathways and permissions.*
25. **[Module 25: Decision Audit Trail](./udios_modules/MODULE_25_DECISION_AUDIT_TRAIL.md)**  
    *Immutable cryptographic logging of all input changes, weights, and votes.*
26. **[Module 26: Post-Decision Review](./udios_modules/MODULE_26_POST_DECISION_REVIEW.md)**  
    *Triggering post-implementation performance metrics tracking vs. baseline expectations.*
27. **[Module 27: Decision Knowledge Graph](./udios_modules/MODULE_27_DECISION_KNOWLEDGE_GRAPH.md)**  
    *Mapping the dynamic network of decisions, showing downstream impacts on architecture and costs.*
28. **[Module 28: Autonomous Decision Agent](./udios_modules/MODULE_28_AUTONOMOUS_DECISION_AGENT.md)**  
    *Execution rules allowing autonomous AI agents to select parameters and run decision loops.*

## 3. General Operating Guidelines

1.  **Strict Gating**: No PR containing structural changes (new cloud infrastructure, new library imports, model changes) will be merged without an approved UDIOS ADR hash in the commit log.
2.  **Continuous Telemetry**: Decisions under review (Module 26) feed performance deviations back into Module 21 (Institutional Memory), dynamically adjusting weights for new proposals.
