# PROJECT VENUS — V0.3: PROBLEM DISCOVERY ENGINE
## The Institutional Problem Validation & Readiness Operating System

**Version**: 0.3  
**Classification**: Institutional Engineering Standard  
**Requirement**: Mandatory gateway approval prior to architectural design, technological selection, or engineering resource allocation.

---

## 1. System Architecture

The **Problem Discovery Engine (V0.3)** is designed to prevent engineering organizations from building technically sound solutions to unvalidated, incorrect, or symptoms-only problems. It acts as an objective validator, turning initial ideas or customer complaints into a verified **Problem Definition Package** that is ready for architectural engineering.

## 2. Modular Engine Index

The Problem Discovery Engine is divided into 13 operational modules, 6 core decision frameworks, an automated AI-agent execution workflow, and 12 output templates. Follow the links below to access each module's complete operating manual:

### 2.1 The 13 Operational Modules
1.  **[Module 1: Idea Intake Engine](./modules/MODULE_1_IDEA_INTAKE.md)**  
    *Intake pipelines, automatic classification (novelty, complexity, impact), and initial confidence score models.*
2.  **[Module 2: Problem Classification Engine](./modules/MODULE_2_PROBLEM_CLASSIFICATION.md)**  
    *Simultaneous multi-vector classification (technical, workflow, compliance, organizational, human).*
3.  **[Module 3: Root Cause Discovery](./modules/MODULE_3_ROOT_CAUSE_DISCOVERY.md)**  
    *Five Whys, Fishbone, Causal Loops, and Fault Tree mappings to isolate symptoms from root and latent causes.*
4.  **[Module 4: Stakeholder Intelligence](./modules/MODULE_4_STAKEHOLDER_INTELLIGENCE.md)**  
    *Incentive matrix generation, fear tracking, and influence mapping for all buying and operating roles.*
5.  **[Module 5: Assumption Discovery](./modules/MODULE_5_ASSUMPTION_DISCOVERY.md)**  
    *Extraction, scoring (evidence vs. impact), and auto-generation of the project Assumption Register.*
6.  **[Module 6: Unknown Discovery Engine](./modules/MODULE_6_UNKNOWN_DISCOVERY.md)**  
    *Mapping of Known-Unknowns and Unknown-Unknowns, uncertainty ceilings, and prioritization algorithms.*
7.  **[Module 7: Constraint Discovery](./modules/MODULE_7_CONSTRAINT_DISCOVERY.md)**  
    *Technical, legal, operational, and geographical constraints; dependency graph specifications.*
8.  **[Module 8: Opportunity Discovery](./modules/MODULE_8_OPPORTUNITY_DISCOVERY.md)**  
    *Identifying hidden opportunities: cost reductions, simplification vectors, and platformization paths.*
9.  **[Module 9: Failure Prediction](./modules/MODULE_9_FAILURE_PREDICTION.md)**  
    *Simulated failure mode prediction: mapping the top 100 failure paths with prevention planning.*
10. **[Module 10: Systems Context](./modules/MODULE_10_SYSTEMS_CONTEXT.md)**  
    *Ecosystem mapping: data flow vectors, API integrations, vendor boundaries, and feedback loop visualizer.*
11. **[Module 11: AI Opportunity Analysis](./modules/MODULE_11_AI_OPPORTUNITY_ANALYSIS.md)**  
    *Objective AI decision matrix: classifying requirements from simple automation to multi-agent planning.*
12. **[Module 12: Success Definition](./modules/MODULE_12_SUCCESS_DEFINITION.md)**  
    *Quantitative success criteria: mapping technical, operational, and financial KPIs before coding.*
13. **[Module 13: Engineering Readiness](./modules/MODULE_13_ENGINEERING_READINESS.md)**  
    *The readiness score calculation and formal gatekeeper checks prior to architectural sign-off.*

## 3. General Operating Guidelines

1.  **Solution Gating**: If any developer, architect, or manager proposes a coding stack or architectural pattern before the Problem Definition Package is approved, the system logs a process failure.
2.  **Continuous Updates**: The Assumption and Risk registers are living documents that continue to update throughout the system's operational lifecycle.
