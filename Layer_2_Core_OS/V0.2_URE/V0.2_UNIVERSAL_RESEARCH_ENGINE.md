## 1. System Overview & Executive Architecture

The **Universal Research Engine (V0.2)** is the primary operational gating mechanism of Project VENUS. It is a systematic process designed to eliminate uncertainty and mitigate commercial, technical, and compliance risk before software architecture, coding, or deployment begins.

The Research Engine treats research not as information gathering, but as **risk and uncertainty elimination**.

## 2. Modular Engine Index

The Universal Research Engine is divided into 10 executable stages and 15 reusable deliverable templates. To access the operational guidelines and templates, follow the links below:

### 2.1 The 10 Research Stages
1.  **[Stage 1: Problem Discovery](./stages/STAGE_1_PROBLEM_DISCOVERY.md)**  
    *Objective: Separate symptoms from root causes using the Five Whys, Jobs-to-be-Done, and failure archaeology.*
2.  **[Stage 2: Market Intelligence](./stages/STAGE_2_MARKET_INTELLIGENCE.md)**  
    *Objective: Quantitative analysis of TAM/SAM/SOM, geopolitical dynamics, supply chain constraints, and macro shifts.*
3.  **[Stage 3: User Intelligence](./stages/STAGE_3_USER_INTELLIGENCE.md)**  
    *Objective: Comprehensive persona mapping, cognitive friction profiling, switching cost calculations, and buying committees.*
4.  **[Stage 4: Competitive Intelligence](./stages/STAGE_4_COMPETITIVE_INTELLIGENCE.md)**  
    *Objective: Technical architecture disassembly, commercial teardowns, and defensive moat analysis of competitors.*
5.  **[Stage 5: Technology Intelligence](./stages/STAGE_5_TECHNOLOGY_INTELLIGENCE.md)**  
    *Objective: First-principles decision matrices for programming languages, database structures, message queues, and cloud hosts.*
6.  **[Stage 6: AI Research Framework](./stages/STAGE_6_AI_RESEARCH_FRAMEWORK.md)**  
    *Objective: Rigorous verification of frontier/open LLMs, inference latencies, grounding layers, and safety boundaries.*
7.  **[Stage 7: Economic Research](./stages/STAGE_7_ECONOMIC_RESEARCH.md)**  
    *Objective: Operational cost modeling, cloud/inference compute forecasts, LTV/CAC calculations, and architectural correlation.*
8.  **[Stage 8: Legal & Compliance Research](./stages/STAGE_8_LEGAL_COMPLIANCE_RESEARCH.md)**  
    *Objective: Regulatory readiness matrices (GDPR, SOC 2, HIPAA, PCI DSS, AI Act) and licensing compliance.*
9.  **[Stage 9: Risk Intelligence](./stages/STAGE_9_RISK_INTELLIGENCE.md)**  
    *Objective: Standard risk taxonomy classification across technical, financial, operational, and personnel metrics.*
10. **[Stage 10: Decision Readiness](./stages/STAGE_10_DECISION_READINESS.md)**  
    *Objective: The formal exit gate checking scorecard validations prior to software engineering approval.*

## 3. General Execution Rules

1.  **Strict Sequencing**: Stage 1 must complete before Stage 2 can begin. Stage 10 is the ultimate gate.
2.  **Verifiable Evidence**: Every data point in a deliverable must be backed by a primary or validated secondary source. Assertions without source references are ignored.
3.  **The "Uncertainty Ceiling"**: If any stage receives a confidence rating lower than 80%, the entire pipeline halts until further research eliminates the blocker.
