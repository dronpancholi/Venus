# Engine: Competitive Intelligence

## 1. Context & Strategy

### 1.1 Purpose
The Competitive Intelligence Engine tracks competitor products, pricing metrics, funding levels, GTM moves, and architectural weak spots to identify competitive advantages.

### 1.2 Philosophy
Understand the enemy. Auditing competitor technical vulnerabilities allows us to design architectures that bypass their scaling limits.
