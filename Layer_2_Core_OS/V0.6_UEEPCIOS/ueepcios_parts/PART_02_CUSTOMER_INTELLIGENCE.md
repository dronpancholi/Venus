# Part 02 — Customer Intelligence

## 1. Context & Strategy
Customer Intelligence models target Ideal Customer Profiles (ICP), buying committee dynamics, user psychology, Jobs-to-be-Done (JTBD), and lifetime customer metrics.

---

## 2. Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) Taxonomy
We segment buyers across four profiles:
*   **Enterprise**: High contract value (ACV > $100K/yr), complex security compliance limits, 6-12 month sales cycles.
*   **SMB**: Faster onboarding, self-serve or light sales (ACV $10K-$50K/yr).
*   **Startup**: Extremely price sensitive, rapid adoption, high churn.
*   **Government**: Long procurement cycles, strict regulatory constraints.

---

## 3. Financial Metrics (CAC & LTV)
We track efficiency using the standard customer value formula:

\[LTV = \frac{Average\_Revenue\_Per\_Account \times Gross\_Margin}{Churn\_Rate}\]

Enforced guardrail target: **LTV / CAC Ratio >= 3.0**.
