# Part 12 — Enterprise Readiness

## 1. Context & Strategy
Enterprise Readiness details SOC2 compliance, ISO certifications, GDPR/HIPAA boundaries, SLA templates, procurement protocols, and legal frameworks to secure high-value contracts.

---

## 2. Compliance Boundaries Matrix
*   **SOC2 Type II**: Validates system security controls, encryption, and log audits over 6-12 month review periods.
*   **GDPR / CCPA**: Enforces strict user data deletion and localized hosting subnet rules.
*   **HIPAA**: Dictates 100% PII data encryption at rest and in transit (requires BAA contract signatures with providers).

---

## 3. SLA Targets & Penalties
*   **Availability Target**: 99.9% uptime SLA.
*   **Downtime Penalty**: Enforce refund credit allocations if monthly availability drops below target.

```
[System Availability Drops < 99.9%] ──► [Trigger Service Credit Penalty] ──► [10% Refund to Client]
```
