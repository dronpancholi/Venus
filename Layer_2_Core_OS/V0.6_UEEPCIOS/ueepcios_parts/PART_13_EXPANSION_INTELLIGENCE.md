# Part 13 — Expansion Intelligence

## 1. Context & Strategy
Expansion Intelligence structures international business growth, covering multi-currency routing, local tax compliance, data localization regulations, and translation workflows.

---

## 2. Localization Vectors

| Vector | Requirement | Technical Impact |
|---|---|---|
| **Multi-Currency** | Support local checkout pricing | Integrate Stripe multi-currency router |
| **Tax Compliance** | Automated VAT/Sales Tax calculations| Integrate Stripe Tax / TaxJar API |
| **Data Residency** | Dynamic data storage routing | Deploy isolated localized database subnets |
| **Localization** | Multi-language translation support | Implement i18n key-value templates |

---

## 3. Expansion Sequencing
Establish a priority roadmap for regional expansion:
*   *Phase 1*: US / Canada (standard baseline).
*   *Phase 2*: EU / UK (requires GDPR compliance and local hosting).
*   *Phase 3*: LATAM / APAC (requires localized payment methods like Pix, Boleto).
