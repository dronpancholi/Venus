# Engine: Acquisition Readiness

## 1. Context & Strategy

### 1.1 Purpose
The Acquisition Readiness Engine maps potential corporate buyers to our IP assets, customers list, and technology stack.

### 1.2 Philosophy
Exits must be structured. The engine compares strategic alignment weights to identify high-probability acquirers.

---

## 2. Buyer Alignment Calculation
The engine evaluates alignment across three variables (scored 1-5):

\[Alignment\_Score = (Strategic\_Fit \times 0.4) + (Tech\_Overlap \times 0.3) + (Talent\_Match \times 0.3)\]

Proposals with Alignment Score >= 4.0 are prioritized in the exit buyer matrix.

---

## 3. Acquisition Readiness Checklist & Exit Criteria
*   [ ] Structured Strategic Buyer Matrix.
*   [ ] Checked comparable transaction multiples.
*   *Exit Criteria*: Acquisition Readiness Report approved.
