# Part 04 — Business Model Intelligence

## 1. Context & Strategy
Business Model Intelligence determines the commercial transaction engine: SaaS, Marketplaces, Usage-Based, Credit/Consumption, Open Core, or Hybrid subscriptions.

---

## 2. Business Model Comparison

| Model Class | Revenue Stability | Growth Velocity | Net Revenue Retention (NRR) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **SaaS Subscription**| High (predictable recurring)| Medium | 110-120% |
| **Usage-Based (API)** | Low (scales with traffic) | High | 130-140% (high expansion) |
| **Open Core** | Medium | Low (requires large scale)| 100-110% |
| **Marketplace Fee** | Low | High | Variable |

---

## 3. Net Revenue Retention (NRR) Formula
To ensure healthy long-term growth, the business model must target a high NRR:

\[NRR = \frac{Starting\_ARR + Expansion - Contraction - Churn}{Starting\_ARR}\]

Target baseline: **NRR >= 110%** for SMB SaaS; **NRR >= 120%** for Enterprise.

---
