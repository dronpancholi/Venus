# Part 10 — Investment Intelligence

## 1. Context & Strategy
Investment Intelligence structures VC/PE memos, due diligence checklists, risk matrices, and valuation models (ARR multiples, DCF) matching the Project OMEGA standard.

---

## 2. Valuation Modeling
Valuation projections utilize two standard methodologies:
*   **Discounted Cash Flow (DCF)**: Calculates net present value (NPV) based on expected cash flows discounted at a weighted cost of capital (WACC).
*   **Multiple-Based Valuation**: Applies prevailing industry ARR multiples (e.g. 10x - 20x ARR) based on growth rate:

\[Valuation = Current\_ARR \times ARR\_Multiple\]

---

## 3. Due Diligence Areas
*   **Technology Risk**: Technical debt, single points of failure, lack of tests.
*   **Commercial Risk**: Customer concentration risk (e.g. single customer represents > 30% ARR).
*   **Compliance Risk**: Unmitigated GDPR, SOC2 compliance exposures.

---
