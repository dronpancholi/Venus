# Part 01 — Global Market Intelligence

## 1. Context & Strategy
Global Market Intelligence models market size, growth rates, industry maturity levels, regional opportunities, and competitive density prior to committing capital.

---

## 2. Market Sizing Calculations
We structure market analysis across three tiers:
*   **Total Addressable Market (TAM)**: Total global revenue opportunity (e.g., $10B/yr).
*   **Serviceable Addressable Market (SAM)**: The share of TAM targeted by our product profile (e.g., $1.5B/yr).
*   **Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM)**: The share of SAM we can realistically capture in 3-5 years (e.g., $150M/yr).

\[SOM = SAM \times Target\_Market\_Share\_Percentage\]

---

## 3. Technology Adoption & Density
*   *Technology Adoption Curve*: Early Adopters / Chasm / Early Majority.
*   *Competitive Density (1-5)*: 1: Green field. 3: Established SaaS players. 5: Hyper-saturated market with zero margins.

---

## 4. Market Intelligence Checklist
*   [ ] Calculated TAM/SAM/SOM targets.

*   [ ] Audited competitor density.
*   [ ] Identified adjacent and expansion markets.
