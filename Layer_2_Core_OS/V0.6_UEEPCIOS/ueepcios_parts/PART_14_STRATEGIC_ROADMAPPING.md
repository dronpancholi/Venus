# Part 14 — Strategic Roadmapping

## 1. Context & Strategy
Strategic Roadmapping models 3-year, 5-year, and 10-year platform evolution, network effects, partner ecosystems, and future acquisition horizons.

---

## 2. Platform Strategy Horizon
*   **Year 1-3 (Point Solution)**: Build a high-performance specific utility (e.g. automated backlinking search).
*   **Year 3-5 (Platform API)**: Expose internal features as reusable developer APIs, creating integration ecosystems.
*   **Year 5-10 (Industry Protocol)**: Establish the default registry standard, creating high switching costs.
