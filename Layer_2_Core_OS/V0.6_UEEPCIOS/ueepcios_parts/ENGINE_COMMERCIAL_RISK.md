# Engine: Commercial Risk

## 1. Context & Strategy

### 1.1 Purpose
The Commercial Risk Engine identifies, scores, and mitigates business model risks, customer concentration threats, regulatory changes, and pricing failures.

### 1.2 Philosophy
Business risk is a default state. We analyze risk indicators (probability, impact, recoverability) to assign risk mitigation strategies.

---

## 2. Risk Score calculation
The risk score is calculated as:

\[Risk\_Score = \frac{Probability \times Impact \times Recoverability}{5.0}\]

If Risk Score >= 8.0, the business model proposal is blocked, requiring adjustments to pricing structures or customer distributions.

---

## 3. Commercial Risk Checklist & Exit Criteria
*   [ ] Checked customer concentration ratios.
*   [ ] Audited regulatory and GDPR data boundary compliance.
*   *Exit Criteria*: Commercial Risk Register updated and approved.
