# Engine: Commercial Optimization

## 1. Context & Strategy

### 1.1 Purpose
The Commercial Optimization Engine audits active metrics (CAC, LTV, churn, margins) to identify high-leverage optimizations.

### 1.2 Philosophy
Continuous optimization is the core margin driver. The engine runs sweeps to recommend cost reductions and pricing adjustments.
