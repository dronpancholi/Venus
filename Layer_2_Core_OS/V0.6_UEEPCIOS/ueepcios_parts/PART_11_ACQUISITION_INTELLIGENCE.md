# Part 11 — Acquisition Intelligence

## 1. Context & Strategy
Acquisition Intelligence maps potential corporate buyers (strategic fit, technology values, talent values, IP moats) and models comparable acquisition multiples.

---

## 2. Strategic Buyer Profile

| Buyer Category | Primary Motivation | Exit Valuation Driver | Target Multiples |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Strategic Competitor**| Eliminating competition, scaling GTM | ARR, market share scale | 10x - 20x ARR |
| **Enterprise Platform**| Product catalog expansion | Technology, IP, customer overlap| 8x - 15x ARR |
| **Private Equity (PE)** | Cash flow optimization | EBITDA, operational efficiency | 4x - 8x EBITDA |

---

## 3. Exit Valuation Calculations
Exit valuations are modeled across comparable metrics:
*   *Asset-based value*: Calculated as replacement engineering cost + IP patents value.
*   *Multiple-based value*: Based on ARR growth targets at Month 36.
