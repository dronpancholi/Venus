# Part 08 — Go-To-Market Intelligence

## 1. Context & Strategy
Go-To-Market (GTM) Intelligence structures launch tactics, user acquisition channels (PLG, SLG, SEO, Inbound, Outbound), partnerships, and developer relations metrics.

---

## 2. GTM Motion Comparison
*   **Product-Led Growth (PLG)**: Self-serve onboarding, low CAC, high volume, virality-dependent.
*   **Sales-Led Growth (SLG)**: Direct enterprise sales, high ACV, high CAC, longer sales cycles.
*   **Developer Relations (DevRel)**: Open-source community adoption, organic developers advocating to executives.

---

## 3. Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) Efficiency
We track marketing and sales channel spend using the CAC formula:

\[CAC = \frac{Sales\_Spend + Marketing\_Spend}{New\_Accounts\_Acquired}\]

*   *Target Payback Period*: **CAC Payback <= 12 months** (calculated as CAC / MRR).
