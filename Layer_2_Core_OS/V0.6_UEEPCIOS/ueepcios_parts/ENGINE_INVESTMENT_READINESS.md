# Engine: Investment Readiness

## 1. Context & Strategy

### 1.1 Purpose
The Investment Readiness Engine audits due diligence folders, investor memos, equity share schedules, and financial projection registers.

### 1.2 Philosophy
Due diligence readiness accelerates investment. Standardizing compliance checks ensures the corporate package is clean and ready for institutional investor audits.
