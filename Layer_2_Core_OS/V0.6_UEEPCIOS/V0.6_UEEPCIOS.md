## 3. General Operating Guidelines

1.  **Financial Gating**: No product feature branch containing structural pricing alterations or cloud spends will be merged without an approved UEEPCIOS margin audit signature in the git log.
2.  **Continuous Economics Telemetry**: Live cloud billing alerts and customer retention metrics (Part 02) feed back into the Unit Economics Engine (Module 20), updating margins.
