## 2. Risk Scoring Methodology
Risks are assessed using a standard probability and impact scale from 1 (lowest) to 5 (highest):

\[Risk\_Score = Probability \times Impact\]

| Risk Score Range | Risk Level | Action Required |
|---|---|---|
| **16 - 25** | **Critical** | Immediate mitigation plan, executive sponsor escalation, launch gate hold. |
| **8 - 15** | **High** | Monthly monitoring, active mitigation tasks assigned. |
| **1 - 7** | **Medium/Low** | Quarterly monitoring, logged for status updates. |
