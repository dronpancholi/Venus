## 3. Exit Multiple Simulation Formula
Exit valuation simulations utilize a standard ARR multiple scaling formula, factoring in growth and churn discount modifiers:

\[Exit\_Valuation = ARR_{Exit} \times M_{Base} \times (1 + G_{YoY}) \times (1 - Churn_{Net})\]

Where:
*   \(ARR_{Exit}\): Projected Exit Run-Rate ARR.
*   \(M_{Base}\): Base Industry Multiple (Default: **10x**).
*   \(G_{YoY}\): Year-over-Year Growth Rate percentage.
*   \(Churn_{Net}\): Net Revenue Churn percentage.
