## 2. Mathematical Formulations & Performance Models
Executive Leadership Index ($ELI$) represents the evaluation metrics:
$$ELI = w_1 \times P_{growth} + w_2 \times ESG_{compliance} + w_3 \times Succession\_Score$$
where weights are structured as:
$$w_1 = 0.50,\ w_2 = 0.25,\ w_3 = 0.25$$
Financial growth factor is defined as:
$$P_{growth} = \frac{EBITDA_{current} - EBITDA_{baseline}}{EBITDA_{baseline}}$$

## 5. Institutional Execution Checklist
### 5.1 Pre-Execution Phase
- [ ] Validate target executive succession allocations with the Board of Directors. - [ ] Perform regulatory and conflicts clearance on candidate pool.

### 5.2 Execution Phase
- [ ] Execute the executive board evaluation and strategic presentation rounds. - [ ] Audit candidate's operational performance records.

### 5.3 Post-Execution Phase
- [ ] Publish promotion outcomes to SEC and regulatory filing systems if necessary. - [ ] Transition operational credentials and signing authority limits.

### 5.4 Exception / Rollback Phase
- [ ] Withdraw nomination if review reveals corporate policy or SEC compliance violations. - [ ] Log executive pathway failure record.
