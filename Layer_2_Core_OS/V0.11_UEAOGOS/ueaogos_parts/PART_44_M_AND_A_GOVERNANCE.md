## 1. Executive Summary
This document defines the corporate mergers and acquisitions (M&A) governance standard. It governs the target search, evaluation, due diligence, and integration process.

## 2. Core Pillars & Strategic Principles
All enterprise systems, business processes, and governance systems associated with M&A Governance must conform to the following three strategic pillars:
1. **Due Diligence Verification: Technical, financial, and legal health must be audited before acquisition.**
2. **Integration Speed: Merged entities must adopt the Venus Operating System in under 90 days.**
3. **Value Realization: Synergy plans must be reviewed monthly against targets.**

## 3. Mathematical Formulations & Actuarial Models
Acquisition decisions require that the Synergy Value ($S$) satisfies the relation:

$$S = V_{AB} - (V_A + V_B) - C_{integration}$$

Where:
- $V_{AB}$ is the post-merger value of the combined enterprise.
- $V_A$ is the independent value of company A (acquirer).
- $V_B$ is the purchase cost of company B (target).
- $C_{integration}$ is the total cost of systems integration.

Governance constraint:
$$S > 0.15 \times (V_A + V_B)$$

## 5. Institutional Compliance Checklists
To verify compliance with the constitutional rules of Project Venus, teams must execute and sign off on the following operations checklists:

### 5.1 Pre-Execution Phase
- [ ] Perform conflict of interest checks for all deal team members.
- [ ] Sign mutual NDA documents with the target entity.
- [ ] Confirm that role-based permissions are assigned and validated.
- [ ] Verify telemetry logging is active and writing to the designated audit store.

### 5.2 Execution Phase
- [ ] Perform physical, technical, and financial due diligence audit.
- [ ] Model the combination benefits using the M&A Integration Assessor.
- [ ] Collect transaction timestamps and metrics for real-time monitoring.
- [ ] Sign off on execution artifacts with authorized cryptographic keys.

### 5.3 Post-Execution Phase
- [ ] Draft the final asset purchase agreement.
- [ ] Deploy the systems integration teams post-closing.
- [ ] Verify that all metrics are recorded in the central data lake.
- [ ] Archive the execution documentation for regulatory audit compliance.

### 5.4 Exception & Rollback Phase
- [ ] Trigger transaction exit clauses if due diligence uncovers material misrepresentations.
- [ ] Log the transaction exit incident.
- [ ] Trigger security incidents and log escalation logs.
- [ ] Restore target systems to the last known stable configuration.
