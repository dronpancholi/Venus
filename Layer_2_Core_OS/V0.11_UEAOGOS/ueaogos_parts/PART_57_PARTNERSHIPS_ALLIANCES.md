All enterprise systems, business processes, and governance systems associated with Partnerships & Alliances must conform to the following three strategic pillars:

## 3. Mathematical Formulations & Actuarial Models
Partnership profitability is analyzed using the Partner Value Index ($PVI$):

$$PVI = \frac{Revenue_{sourced} + Revenue_{influenced}}{Cost_{enablement}}$$

Where:
- $Revenue_{sourced}$ is the revenue directly closed through partner channels.
- $Revenue_{influenced}$ is the revenue influenced by joint partner activities.
- $Cost_{enablement}$ is the cost of marketing and training support provided to the partner.

Strategic alliance targets require:
$$PVI \ge 4.0$$
