## 2. Mathematical Formulations
Change Readiness Score ($CRS_{change}$) quantifies the probability of successful change adoption:
$$CRS_{change} = w_s \cdot S_{sponsor} + w_a \cdot A_{align} + w_t \cdot T_{training} - \delta \cdot R_{resistance}$$
Where:
- $S_{sponsor} \in [0, 1]$ is the executive sponsorship index from surveys.
- $A_{align} \in [0, 1]$ is the strategic alignment survey score.
- $T_{training} \in [0, 1]$ is the training readiness completion score.
- $R_{resistance} \in [0, 1]$ is the measured employee resistance index.
- $w_s, w_a, w_t$ are normalized weights, and $\delta \ge 0.15$ is the resistance penalty factor.

## 5. Institutional Checklist
This checklist guides the execution life cycle of the engine. All actions must be verified by the executing agent.

### 5.1 Pre-Execution Phase
- Verify that surveys are distributed to targeted business units.
- Retrieve previous survey response baselines.
- Clear survey aggregation node cache.

### 5.2 Execution Phase
- Execute statistical survey response aggregations.
- Run data checks using ChangeReadinessPayload validation schema.
- Calculate the Change Readiness Score ($CRS_{change}$).

### 5.3 Post-Execution Phase
- Deliver readiness reports to project steering committee.
- Adjust system rollout timelines where required.
- Archive survey statistics in change management database.

### 5.4 Exception & Rollback Phase
- If CRS falls below 0.70, postpone system migration activities.
- Hold changes on deployment schedules.
- Revert migration status flag to 'Under Planning'.
