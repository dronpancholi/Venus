## 2. Mathematical Formulations
Clause Risk Index ($CRI$) represents the aggregate liability weight of contract deviations:
$$CRI = \sum_{a=1}^{A} w_a \cdot R_a(C_a) \cdot I_{indemnity}$$
Where:
- $A$ is the total count of analyzed legal clauses.
- $w_a$ is the risk weight assigned to clause type $a$.
- $R_a(C_a) \in [0, 10]$ is the deviation severity rating of clause $C_a$ compared to template terms.
- $I_{indemnity} \in \{1.0, 2.5\}$ is a multiplier penalty indicating presence of adverse indemnity positions.

## 5. Institutional Checklist
This checklist guides the execution life cycle of the engine. All actions must be verified by the executing agent.

### 5.1 Pre-Execution Phase
- Ingest document source text through text parser.
- Match text against approved corporate contract templates.
- Load risk weighting configurations.

### 5.2 Execution Phase
- Execute semantic parser to identify individual legal terms.
- Validate extracted terms against LegalAuditPayload schema.
- Calculate the Clause Risk Index ($CRI$).

### 5.3 Post-Execution Phase
- Generate legal redline document copy automatically.
- Send compliance status report to the legal operations portal.
- Archive contract draft in legal repository.

### 5.4 Exception & Rollback Phase
- If CRI is greater than 6.5, halt automated approval workflows.
- Route contract directly to General Counsel queue for manual triage.
- Revert negotiation state indicator to 'Under Review'.
