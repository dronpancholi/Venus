## 3. Mathematical Formulations & Actuarial Models
Corporate compliance level is measured using the Ethical Compliance Index ($ECI$):

$$ECI = 1 - \frac{Cases_{breached}}{Employees_{total}}$$

Where:
- $Cases_{breached}$ is the count of confirmed ethics violations in the fiscal year.
- $Employees_{total}$ is the average headcount during the fiscal year.

The target performance requirement is:
$$ECI \ge 0.999$$
