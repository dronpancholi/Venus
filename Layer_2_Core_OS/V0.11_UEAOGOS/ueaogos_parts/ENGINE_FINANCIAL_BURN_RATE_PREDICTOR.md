## 2. Mathematical Formulations
Runway Extension Coefficient ($REC$) defines the multiplier of forecasted operational viability:
$$REC = \frac{C_{cash\_reserve}}{B_{forecasted} + \sigma_B} \cdot \left(1 - \frac{\Delta R_{fixed}}{R_{current}}\right)$$
Where:
- $C_{cash\_reserve}$ is the active cash reserves balance.
- $B_{forecasted}$ is the forecasted monthly operational cash burn.
- $\sigma_B$ is the standard deviation of historical burn rates.
- $\Delta R_{fixed}$ is the planned reduction in fixed expenditures.
- $R_{current}$ is the current recurring revenue rate.

## 5. Institutional Checklist
This checklist guides the execution life cycle of the engine. All actions must be verified by the executing agent.

### 5.1 Pre-Execution Phase
- Fetch cash accounts balances from unified banking registers.
- Run data completeness checks on previous month's ledger books.
- Retrieve latest fixed-cost reduction targets.

### 5.2 Execution Phase
- Execute financial forecast simulation modeling.
- Run forecast outputs through the FinancialBurnPayload schema validator.
- Calculate the Runway Extension Coefficient ($REC$).

### 5.3 Post-Execution Phase
- Export runway projections into standard Board reporting decks.
- Publish financial alerts to executive stakeholders.
- Record prediction metrics to forecast database.

### 5.4 Exception & Rollback Phase
- If REC indicates a runway under 6.0 months, trigger priority capital alert.
- Freeze all non-essential operational expenditure lines.
- Revert spending levels to standard emergency budget limits.
