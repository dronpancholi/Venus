## 2. Mathematical Formulations
Total Enterprise Carbon Footprint ($CO_2e$) represents aggregate emissions:
$$CO_2e = \sum_{s=1}^{3} \left( \sum_{e=1}^{E_s} Q_{e, s} \cdot EF_{e, s} \right)$$
Where:
- $Q_{e, s}$ is the quantity of energy/materials consumed in emissions Scope $s$.
- $EF_{e, s}$ is the carbon equivalent emissions factor for energy source $e$.
- $E_s$ is the number of distinct emission sources tracked in Scope $s$.
- $s \in \{1, 2, 3\}$ designates the carbon disclosure Scope context.

## 5. Institutional Checklist
This checklist guides the execution life cycle of the engine. All actions must be verified by the executing agent.

### 5.1 Pre-Execution Phase
- Retrieve corporate utility, travel, and shipment ledger entries.
- Load verified emissions factors databases.
- Check data integrity of server farm energy reports.

### 5.2 Execution Phase
- Compute carbon equivalency factors for all entries.
- Run data checks using CarbonAuditPayload validation schema.
- Calculate the Total Enterprise Carbon Footprint ($CO_2e$).

### 5.3 Post-Execution Phase
- Generate official ESG report compliance draft files.
- Publish emissions statistics to global regulatory bodies.
- Store audit calculation trails in secure storage.

### 5.4 Exception & Rollback Phase
- If carbon metrics exceed regulatory thresholds, raise emergency ESG risk alarm.
- Apply immediate carbon offsets purchases dynamically.
- Revert target operational transport methods to low-carbon options.
