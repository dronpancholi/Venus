## 3. Mathematical Formulations & Actuarial Models
Enterprise adaptability is measured using the Future-Proofing Index ($FPI$):

$$FPI = w_a \cdot Adaptability + w_i \cdot Innovation - w_l \cdot Legacy$$

Where:
- $Adaptability$ is the process agility score ($0 \le Adaptability \le 1.0$).
- $Innovation$ is the rate of new technology adoption ($0 \le Innovation \le 1.0$).
- $Legacy$ is the ratio of outdated systems to total systems ($0 \le Legacy \le 1.0$).
- $w_a, w_i, w_l$ are weights where $w_a + w_i + w_l = 1.0$ (calibrated as $0.4, 0.45, 0.15$).

The design target mandates:
$$FPI \ge 0.85$$
