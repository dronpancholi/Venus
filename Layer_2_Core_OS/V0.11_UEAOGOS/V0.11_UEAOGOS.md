## 1. Governance Constitution

All enterprise systems, business processes, C-suite operations, and organizational design patterns under Project Venus must satisfy these seven governing rules:

1. **Conway's Law Realignment**: System architecture must match logical communication boundaries. No cross-boundary database sharing or unmapped inter-departmental tool dependencies.
2. **Dynamic KPI/OKR Auditing**: All organizational key results and performance indicators must have telemetry instrumentation. No subjective performance grading.
3. **Continuous Lean Process Profiling**: Every business process must map to a BPMN workflow and undergo weekly cycle-time bottleneck analyses.
4. **Least-Privilege Role Mapping**: Career ladders, project access permissions, and tool credentials must map strictly to active role classifications.
5. **Vendor Risk Isolation**: All third-party tools, vendor services, and external dependencies must satisfy a minimum security risk score ($URQS \ge 0.85$).
6. **Regulatory Compliance by Construction**: Financial, legal, and operational actions must automatically log audit evidence mapped to SOC 2 and ISO 27001 ISMS.
7. **Crisis Decoupling**: Business continuity plans must maintain isolated crisis leadership command centers capable of operations fallback in under 1 hour.
